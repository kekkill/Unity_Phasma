## Implemented systems
- Player movement/look/crouch/sprint, per-participant input action instances, interaction UI and one held item.
- Rooms/temperature, doors/lights, EMF/UV/writing evidence and motion sensor.
- Ghost profile, vision/hearing/memory, anger, probabilistic hunts, chase and room search.
- GhostNavigation owns NavMesh movement; RuntimeNavigationWorld explicitly bakes floor/walls and gives the moving door a carving obstacle. Three connected zones: Bedroom ↔ Corridor ↔ Kitchen. Corridor is not a home ghost room. Roaming stays in a room; room transitions use physical openings. Blocked/stalled transitions fall back to reachable local search; AI does not open doors.
- Alive/Dead state, local participant registry, dead-target filtering, presentation/control disabling, line-of-sight attack, all-dead completion and restart. These are local foundations, not networking or voice transport.
- Defensive items (feature/defensive-items): Dropped/Held/Placed item states, all child colliders disabled while held, R/F placement preview, placed-only writing book/motion sensor. Cross protects in hand and when placed nearby, saves the breaking attack, breaks by profile/anger, repels hunting and supports immune types. Salt container (5 portions) places piles with profile-specific avoidance/scattering/footprints/ignore rules and dynamic Salt NavMesh area updates. Optional cyan ghost debug silhouette can be hidden in Inspector.
- Both gameplay scenes include placement, debug visual, salt reaction and a primitive defensive-item setup. The old Interaction-blocking ProtectionWard is preserved but inactive. Example FootprintGhostProfile and CrossImmuneGhostProfile are supplied alongside existing profiles.
- Latest batch (feature/evidence-journal): personal JournalState, manual Unknown/Confirmed/Excluded notes for four evidence types, salt/cross observations, public three-type catalog, profile-based filtering and stable-ID final choice. J opens, J/Esc/button closes. JournalScreen is a scrollable local UI; PlayerJournalController owns cursor/input blocking and death cleanup. Both scenes are wired. Choices survive contradictory later notes with a warning. Notes survive closing/death during the round and reset on scene restart. Opening cancels placement; crouch posture is preserved; world/hunts continue. Gameplay blocking is per input instance with multiple owners and a close-frame guard.

## Ownership and limitations
PickupItem owns placement state; CrossItem owns broken state/protection eligibility; SaltContainer owns portions; SaltPile owns intact/scattered state. PlayerItemPlacement owns local input/preview, GhostAttack requests protection before death, GhostHuntController ends/restricts hunts after repel, GhostSaltReaction owns local salt contacts/footprint presentation, GhostProfile holds configurable rules. JournalState owns personal notes/choice, JournalCatalog public type definitions, PlayerJournalController menu/input lifecycle and JournalScreen rendering. The journal never reads the active ghost to reveal the correct type. Future server authority and replication remain necessary.

No multiplayer or full mission loop. The journal is implemented but pending local validation; final choice does not yet finish/evaluate a mission. All current test profiles have the same four evidence flags, so salt/cross observations distinguish them until different evidence combinations are agreed. Notes are manual; generic EMF activity is not an implemented EMF-5 signature. Journal notes/choices are per participant, not yet shared or persisted between sessions. One held item. No voice/noise occlusion, door-opening AI, actual floating locomotion or final item models. UV/EMF evidence rules need further refinement. Salt rebakes are suitable for this small test layout; async latency and repeated placement must be measured in Unity. Square NavMesh modifier bounds conservatively approximate circular salt piles; escape is allowed if salt is poured under the ghost. Inventory/placement validation is local; it must be made authoritative before online play. Primitive visuals are for testing. Cross probability/radius and salt portions are starting balance values, not fixed design requirements.
